# Voicebox

**[Open Voicebox](https://dreamtraderafk.github.io/voicebox/)**

Free text-to-speech in your browser. Type, pick a voice by its sample, press Enter. No signup.

- Kokoro: natural English voices (runs in the browser)
- Piper: 100+ voices, including Hindi
- Device: your browser's built-in voices

Host it yourself: Settings > Pages > Deploy from branch > main / root.
